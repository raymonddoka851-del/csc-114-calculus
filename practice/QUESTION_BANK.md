# CSC 114 — Exam Question Bank

Thirty targeted questions: three per course module. Attempt each one closed-book, show steps, and record your score and next action in the [progress tracker](../PROGRESS_TRACKER.md). Worked solutions are in the [answer key](ANSWER_KEY.md).

## Module 1 — Basic Mathematics

### M01-Q1

Simplify 3/4 − 5/8 and state why the result is rational.

### M01-Q2

Simplify (2x³)(−3x²) and evaluate 2⁻².

### M01-Q3

A population of 300,000 doubles every 20 years. What is its population after 60 years?

## Module 2 — Number Systems and Functions

### M02-Q1

Classify f(x) = x² − 2 as even, odd, or neither. Justify using f(−x).

### M02-Q2

Convert 150° to radians and find sin(150°) and cos(150°).

### M02-Q3

(a) Multiply (2 + 3i)(1 − i). (b) Find the equation of the line through (1, 2) and (2, −1).

## Module 3 — Introduction to Differentiation

### M03-Q1

Differentiate f(x) = 5x⁴ − 3x² + 7x − 6 and find f′(1).

### M03-Q2

Find the tangent line to y = x² + 1 at x = 2.

### M03-Q3

A tank’s volume is V(t) litres. At t = 5, V′(5) = −2.4. Interpret the sign and units if t is measured in minutes.

## Module 4 — Methods of Differentiation

### M04-Q1

Differentiate y = x² sin x.

### M04-Q2

Differentiate y = (x + 1)/(x − 1), stating the domain restriction.

### M04-Q3

Differentiate y = (3x² + 1)⁴.

## Module 5 — Applications of Differentiation

### M05-Q1

Differentiate y = ln(2x + 1) + sin(3x).

### M05-Q2

Find and classify the stationary point of y = −x² + 6x.

### M05-Q3

Use one Newton–Raphson step from x₀ = 1 to approximate a root of f(x) = x³ − 2.

## Module 6 — Integral Calculus

### M06-Q1

### M06-Q2

### M06-Q3

## Module 7 — Applications of Integration

### M07-Q1

Find the area under y = x + 1 from x = 0 to x = 2.

### M07-Q2

A flow rate is R(t) = 200 − 4t litres/minute. Find the volume discharged from t = 0 to t = 10 minutes.

### M07-Q3

A particle has velocity v(t) = t − 2 m/s on [0, 4]. Find its displacement and total distance.

## Module 8 — Substitution and Integration by Parts

### M08-Q1

### M08-Q3

## Module 9 — Partial Fractions

### M09-Q1

### M09-Q2

### M09-Q3

## Module 10 — Mean Value Theorem for Integrals

### M10-Q1

### M10-Q2

### M10-Q3
